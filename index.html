    Why I’m building this

    A useful lesson should be easy to revisit.

    I’m building VideoBrief to help learners turn long recordings into something they can review and keep. The aim is simple: useful notes without an account or a paid subscription, with the original source close enough to check.

    This is an early, English-language beta. YouTube links need a pasted transcript. Uploaded recordings use AI speech recognition on your device; study highlights use sentence selection. I’m improving reliability before adding more features.

    Privacy, downloads & limitations

    VideoBrief does not send your recording or transcript to an AI server and does not save it to an account. Refreshing closes your study session, so download anything you want to keep. The speech model and app files may be cached by your browser. Opening a YouTube video connects to YouTube; loading transcription downloads third-party model files. Those services receive ordinary network requests.

    Only upload media you have permission to use. This tool cannot retrieve arbitrary YouTube captions or download YouTube audio from a link. No paid service or API key is required.
