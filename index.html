    2. Try without looking

    Compare with the source

    You assess your answer after comparing it. VideoBrief does not automatically grade correctness. Progress lasts for this session and is included in your download.

    3. Come back to what was difficult

    1. Today: practise, reveal the reference, and mark the topics you want to revisit.
    2. Tomorrow: retry those topics without checking first.
    3. In three days: mix questions from across the lesson.

    A suggested routine. Download your pack to study offline; there are no automatic reminders.

    Review or edit the transcript

    Rebuilding resets practice progress. Keep timestamps and headings if you want them in the new pack.

    Why I’m building this

    From watching to practising.

    I’m building VideoBrief to help learners do something with a lesson: explain it, test their recall, and notice what needs another look. The original source stays close enough to check, and the pack can be downloaded for offline study.

    This English-language beta uses source passages and practice prompts. It does not rewrite lessons with a language model or automatically grade answers. Recordings use AI speech recognition on your device. On the public site, YouTube lessons currently need a pasted transcript.

    Privacy, downloads & limitations

    VideoBrief does not send your recording or pasted transcript to an AI server or save it to an account. Refreshing closes your study session, including answers and review marks, so download anything you want to keep. The speech model and app files may be cached by your browser. Opening a YouTube video connects to YouTube; transcription downloads third-party model files.

    Only upload media you have permission to use. The optional local helper can request available English captions from YouTube; availability can change or be blocked. It sends the video ID to YouTube and runs only on your computer. No public caption service is connected to this site. No paid service or API key is required.
